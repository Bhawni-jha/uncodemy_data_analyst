Full month-wise curriculum
MONTH 1Excel + Business UnderstandingPhase: Foundation

Excel fundamentals & cleaning

Validation, sorting, duplicates, text to columns, flash fill, missing data.

Formulas & lookups

IF/IFS, SUMIFS, COUNTIFS, VLOOKUP, XLOOKUP, INDEX-MATCH.

Pivots, MIS & dashboards

Pivot tables & charts, KPI tracking, slicers, Power Query intro.

Business fundamentals

Business models, sales & marketing processes, operations.

Metrics & KPIs

Revenue, profit, ROI, CAC, CLV, conversion, retention.

Industry domains

Banking, e-commerce, healthcare, retail, manufacturing.

Outcome: Clean raw data, build MIS reports and Excel dashboards.

MONTH 2SQL & Database ConceptsPhase: Analytics

Database fundamentals

DBMS vs RDBMS, keys, data types, constraints, ER diagrams.

Queries & DML

SELECT, WHERE, ORDER BY, INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE, transactions.

Aggregation & joins

GROUP BY, HAVING; INNER, LEFT, RIGHT, FULL, SELF, CROSS.

Subqueries, views, procedures

Correlated subqueries, CTEs, views, stored procedures, UDFs.

Window functions

ROW_NUMBER, RANK, DENSE_RANK, LEAD, LAG, running totals.

Optimisation for analysts

Indexing, execution plans, dashboard-ready data.

Outcome: Write professional SQL and analyse large business datasets.

MONTH 3Power BI & TableauPhase: Analytics

BI fundamentals

BI architecture, analytics lifecycle, KPIs.

Power BI Desktop & Service

Import, relationships, schemas, publishing & sharing.

DAX

Measures, calculated columns, time intelligence.

Power Query / ETL

Cleaning, merging, appending, shaping data.

Tableau

Data blending, interactive dashboards, Tableau Public.

Storytelling with data

Executive reporting, drill-downs, geo visuals.

Outcome: Build interactive dashboards and executive reports.

MONTH 4Python for Data AnalyticsPhase: Python

Python fundamentals

Variables, loops, functions, data structures, files, APIs.

NumPy & Pandas

DataFrames, Excel/CSV/SQL import, grouping, merging.

Cleaning & EDA

Outliers, standardisation, descriptive stats, correlation.

Visualisation

Matplotlib, Seaborn, Plotly charts.

Automation & reporting

Excel report automation, email reports, scheduled workflows.

Python with SQL

Database connectivity, extraction & automated reporting.

Outcome: Clean, analyse and visualise data in Python; automate reports.

MONTH 5Statistics & Analytical ThinkingPhase: Analytics

Descriptive statistics

Mean, median, mode, variance, std. deviation, quartiles.

Probability

Distributions, conditional probability, uncertainty.

Hypothesis testing

Confidence intervals, significance, t-test, chi-square.

Correlation & regression

Coefficients, linear regression basics, trends.

KPI & performance analysis

Sales, revenue, customer and operational metrics.

Predictive thinking

Forecasting, scenario and root-cause analysis.

Is this course suitable for freshers?
Yes. The program is structured from fundamentals and progressively moves toward practical analytics and AI applications.

No advanced programming background is required to begin; Python is taught as part of the program.

Yes, the program is based around LIVE instructor-led training, with weekday, weekend and target batches.
What is the course duration?
6–7 months, including projects, portfolio building and career preparation.
Which tools will I learn?
Advanced Excel, SQL, Power BI, Tableau, Python (NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn), statistics, and AI tools including ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Gemini and Claude.
Will I work on projects?
Yes, the program includes practical projects and portfolio-building activities.
Will I get a GitHub profile?
Students receive guidance to create and build their GitHub/project portfolio.
Is project deployment covered?
Deployment guidance is included as applicable to the project and technology stack.

Yes. AI tools and Prompt Engineering are integrated into the Data Analytics learning journey.
